## Full description (4000 max)

Ride Forge is a route planner and sat-nav made for motorcycle riders who'd rather take the twisty way.

PLAN RIDES THAT ARE WORTH RIDING
• Choose how you want to ride: Twisty, Scenic or Fastest, for the whole ride or a different style for each leg.
• Tap the map or search to add stops; pins snap onto real roads.
• See how twisty a route is (a 0–10 gauge, bend count and a RideScore), with the route coloured by its bends.
• Little arrows show which way the route goes.

LOOPS IN ONE TAP
• Make a round trip from where you are, from home or from any pin: pick a length or riding time, a direction, or a place to ride through.
• Get a few loops to choose from (best balance, most curvy, another way).
• Loops come home a different way and stay off dead ends.

RIDE IT
• Turn-by-turn directions with voice, your speed and the speed limit.
• Keeps guiding with the screen locked, and shows the ride over the lock screen.
• Wander off the route and it finds the way back, ahead of you.
• Add a stop mid-ride (fuel, food, cafés, pubs, toilets, lookouts) and take it off again.
• Pause for a coffee, and record your rides.

ON THE WAY
• Fuel stations, cafés, food, toilets and lookouts along your route.
• Today's fuel prices (Queensland), with the cheapest marked.
• Sights and mountain passes on the map.
• The weather along your ride, at the time you'll get there, and the climb.

YOURS
• Save routes, export and import GPX, and back everything up.
• No account, no ads, no tracking: your rides stay on your phone.

Ride Forge covers Australia. It's young and growing: send feedback from Settings and help shape it.

Always ride to the road and the conditions: a suggested road may be closed, unsealed or unsuitable.
