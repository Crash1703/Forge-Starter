Ride safely: you're in charge

Ride Forge suggests routes. It can't see the road. Always obey road rules, signs and conditions, and use your own judgement: a suggested road may be closed, unsealed, flooded, under roadworks, private, unsuitable for your bike or your riding, or simply wrong. Don't handle your phone while riding; stop somewhere safe first.

  • Map, road and place information comes from OpenStreetMap and other sources, and can be out of date or wrong.
  • Travel times, distances, twistiness scores and elevation are estimates.
  • Fuel prices come from the Queensland Government's fuel price reporting scheme and can be out of date. Stations may not have the fuel or price shown.
  • Weather is a forecast, not a promise.
