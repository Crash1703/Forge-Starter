Privacy policy

Ride Forge · last updated 30 September 2026

Ride Forge is a motorcycle route planner and navigator. This policy says what information the app uses, where it goes, and what we keep. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, your rides stay on your phone, and we never sell anything about you.

What stays on your phone

  • Your saved routes, recorded rides (including their GPS tracks), home, settings, recent searches and a fuel price token if you add one.
  • We never receive these. A backup (Saved → Back up routes & rides) is a file you keep and share as you choose.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Your location

  • The app uses your phone's location to show where you are, to start routes and round trips from there, to guide you while riding, and to record rides.
  • With your permission, it keeps using location while the screen is off during a ride or a recording, and only then.
  • Your location is sent off the phone only as part of the requests below (for example, planning a route from where you are). It's never sent on its own to track you.

What is sent, and to whom

  • Planning routes: the points of your route go to Ride Forge's route server to work out the roads. If it isn't answering, they go to a free public route server (Valhalla, run by FOSSGIS e.V. in Germany). Our server doesn't store routes. Its technical logs, which can include details of a request, are deleted after at most 30 days.
  • Searching for places, and naming pins: what you type, or where a pin is, goes to Photon (by komoot).
  • The map: map images for the area on screen come from OpenFreeMap (and OpenTopoMap for the Terrain map).
  • Elevation and weather: points along your route go to Open-Meteo.
  • Fuel, cafés, toilets, sights and similar places: the area or route being searched goes to OpenStreetMap's Overpass servers. Photos of sights come from Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons.
  • Queensland fuel prices: fetched through Ride Forge's server, and nothing about you is sent with them. If you add your own fuel price token, the app asks the Queensland Government's fuel price service directly.
  • Updates (Android app): once a day, the app asks GitHub whether there's a newer version.
  • Links you tap, such as "Google Maps ↗", open in your browser, under that site's own policy.

Each of these services receives your internet (IP) address as part of the request, and handles it under its own privacy policy. Ride Forge's server is reached through Cloudflare.

Feedback and error reports

  • When you use Settings → Feedback → Send feedback, we receive your message, the app's version, your phone's model and browser details, the screen size, and the country your connection comes from.
  • When the app hits an error, it sends the same details with a description of the error instead of a message (a few reports at most per session).
  • We never receive your location, routes or rides this way, and we don't keep IP addresses.
  • We use these reports only to fix and improve Ride Forge, and keep them for up to 2 years.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-ups, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share your information for marketing.

Children

Ride Forge is made for adult motorcycle riders and isn't directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can deny or withdraw location permission in your phone's settings (the app then can't show where you are or guide you), delete search history and saved map data in Settings, and delete routes and rides in the app.
  • To ask about, or have us delete, feedback you sent, email us. Say roughly when you sent it and what it said, since we don't know who sent what.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and say so in the app's release notes.
