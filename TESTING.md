# Testing Ride Forge

Thanks for helping test Ride Forge, a motorcycle route planner and
navigator made for twisty roads. It's an early version: things will break,
and hearing about it is the point.

## Install (Android)

1. On your phone, open the latest release:
   https://github.com/Crash1703/Forge-Starter/releases/latest
2. Tap the `RideForge-….apk` file to download it, then open it.
3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap
   **Install**.

Only Android for now; an iPhone version comes later. Maps and routing cover
Australia; fuel prices cover Queensland.

## Updates

When there's a new version, the app shows **"Ride Forge 1.x is out ·
Update"** on the map. Tap it, download the new APK and install it over the
top: your routes and rides are kept.

## What to try

- **Plan a route:** tap the map, or search with the 🔍, to add stops. Try
  the ride styles (Fastest, Scenic, Twisty), for the whole route or per leg.
- **Round trips:** the orange ↻ button makes a loop from where you are, or
  tap a pin → **Round trip** to ride out to it and back.
- **Ride it:** **Ride** gives turn-by-turn directions with voice. Try it on a
  real ride with the phone locked in your pocket or on a mount; add a stop on
  the way (**＋ Stop**, e.g. fuel), and remove it again.
- **Preview ride** (in the ⋯ menu) plays a route at 4× speed without moving.
- **Record** your rides (the red dot) and look at them under **Rides**.

## Tell us what's wrong (or right)

**Settings → Feedback → Send feedback.** Say what you did, what you
expected and what happened; a place or road name helps.

## What the app sends

The full [privacy policy](https://crash1703.github.io/Forge-Starter/privacy.html) and [terms of use](https://crash1703.github.io/Forge-Starter/terms.html) are online; in short:

- **Routes and places:** to plan a route, the stops and route go to Ride
  Forge's route server (or, if it's not answering, the free public one).
  Searches go to Photon, map pictures to OpenFreeMap.
- **Feedback:** your message, the app's version and your phone's model.
  Never your location.
- **Error reports:** when the app hits an error, it sends what went wrong
  (the error message, the app's version and phone model), so it can be
  fixed. No location, routes or rides.
- **Recorded rides, saved routes and home** stay on your phone (use
  **Saved → Back up routes & rides** to keep a copy).
